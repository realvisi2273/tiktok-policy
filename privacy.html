Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Access

NiceUploader only accesses data granted by your explicit authorization through TikTok OAuth:

  • Profile Information (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): Display name, avatar, bio, and Open ID to identify and verify the connected creator account.
  • Channel Statistics (user.info.stats): Follower, following, likes, and video count to show performance summary on your dashboard.
  • Publishing & Media Permissions (video.publish, video.upload, video.list): Temporary tokens to post videos, save drafts, and retrieve published video records for library management.

2. Purpose & Use of Data

We use this information strictly to provide creator dashboard analytics, show upload history, and execute video uploads commanded by you. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication tokens are stored securely and locally on your operating environment. No personal video or analytics data is stored on external servers.

4. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can revoke access and delete your token anytime via the TikTok App (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or by resetting your local application settings.
